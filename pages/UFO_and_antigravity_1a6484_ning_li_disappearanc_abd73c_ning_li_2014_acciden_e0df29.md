## Introduction

Ning Li’s working life did not end with a documented disappearance. According to the most detailed public account of her later years, based on an interview with her son George Guangyu Men, it ended after a devastating road accident in Huntsville in 2014. Li was struck by a vehicle while crossing a street on the University of Alabama in Huntsville (UAH) campus, suffered permanent brain damage, was diagnosed with Alzheimer’s disease shortly afterwards and never returned to work.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Huntsville Business Journal Uncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal</span></span></span>

That chronology matters because Li’s earlier withdrawal from open scientific publication had already created an [information vacuum]({{ 'info-vacuums/' | relative_url }}) around her unconventional gravity research. The 2014 accident therefore arrived after years in which outsiders had struggled to establish what she was doing. Later retellings could easily merge two different events — her earlier disappearance from public science and her later medical withdrawal from working life — into one mysterious narrative. The available evidence instead distinguishes them.

## The 2014 campus accident

The central source for the accident is Noah Logan’s 2023 investigation for the *Huntsville Business Journal*. Logan located Li’s son after finding Li’s 2021 Huntsville obituary and interviewed him about the years that had been missing from public accounts of her life. Men said his mother had remained in the United States and continued working in the defence world rather than disappearing abroad.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Huntsville Business Journal Uncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal</span></span></span>

According to that account, Li continued going to work at Redstone Arsenal until 2014. Then, while crossing a street on the UAH campus, she was struck by a vehicle. The consequences were severe: Logan reports that the accident caused permanent brain damage and that Li received an Alzheimer’s diagnosis shortly afterwards. Most decisively for the question of her career, the article states that she **never returned to work**.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Huntsville Business Journal Uncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal</span></span></span>

The accident also had an extraordinary effect on her family. Li’s husband of 46 years witnessed the collision and, according to their son, suffered a heart attack at the moment he saw the impact. He survived the immediate event but died the following year, in 2015.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Huntsville Business Journal Uncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal</span></span></span>

These details are substantially more specific than Li’s short funeral-home obituary. Berryhill Funeral Home’s obituary for Ning Li<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.berryhillfh.com/obituary/ning-li?lud=4CF765EE88E7526FCBA619C30101F7E6" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: berryhillfh.com">[berryhillfh.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">berryhillfh.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Berryhill Funeral Home Ning Li Obituary</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Berryhill Funeral HomeNing Li Obituary July 27, 2021 - Berryhill Funeral Home &amp; CrematoryJuly 27, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 27, 2021</span></span></span> confirms that she remained in the Huntsville area, died on 27 July 2021 and was survived by her son and grandchildren, but it does not describe the accident or her years of disability.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.berryhillfh.com/obituary/ning-li?lud=4CF765EE88E7526FCBA619C30101F7E6" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: berryhillfh.com">[Berryhill Funeral Home]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">berryhillfh.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Berryhill Funeral Home Ning Li Obituary</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Berryhill Funeral HomeNing Li Obituary July 27, 2021 - Berryhill Funeral Home &amp; CrematoryJuly 27, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 27, 2021</span></span></span> The accident chronology entered the wider public record principally through Men’s later interview.

That distinction is important when assessing evidential strength. There is strong family-sourced reporting that the collision occurred and permanently disabled Li, reinforced by later secondary accounts that trace the information back to the 2023 investigation. But the publicly accessible sources found for this account do not supply an original 2014 collision report, name the driver, give the precise date of the accident or document a criminal finding of deliberate harm. Claims that go beyond the reported accident therefore require evidence that the present public record does not provide.

## Her injuries ended the career that public records could not explain

The accident explains the final, irreversible end of Li’s working life, but it should not be confused with the earlier end of her *public* scientific career. Those are separate points on the timeline.

Li had already largely disappeared from open publication years before the collision. The *Huntsville Business Journal* reports that after leaving UAH and moving into defence-related work, she obtained a top-secret clearance and became unable to discuss her work openly. Her son recalled that she had previously enjoyed publishing her findings but became much quieter after entering [classified work]({{ 'classified-work/' | relative_url }}).
