## Introduction

The strongest evidence against the idea that physicist Ning Li literally “disappeared” is not a newly released government file but an ordinary family chronology. In 2023, Li’s son, George Guangyu Men, told the *[Huntsville]({{ 'huntsville/' | relative_url }}) Business Journal* that after a serious traffic accident in 2014 left his mother with permanent brain damage, he brought her home and cared for her himself for roughly six years. Li never returned to work and died in Alabama on 27 July 2021.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

That account matters because the long-running mystery around Li rested heavily on confusing public silence with physical disappearance. Her scientific publications had largely stopped years earlier, and her defence-related work remained opaque. But Men’s account supplies a concrete final phase of her life: accident, disability, family care and death. An independently published funeral-home notice confirms that Li died in the Huntsville area and identifies George Men as her son.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.berryhillfh.com/guestbook/ning-li" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: berryhillfh.com">[Berryhill Funeral Home]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">berryhillfh.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Berryhill Funeral Home Ning Li Obituary</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Berryhill Funeral HomeNing Li Obituary July 27, 2021 - Berryhill Funeral Home &amp; CrematoryJuly 27, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 27, 2021</span></span></span> The caregiving testimony therefore does not solve the separate mystery of what Li did in classified research; it substantially challenges the narrower claim that Li herself simply vanished.

## What Men says happened after the accident

According to Men’s account, the decisive event came in 2014. Li was struck by a vehicle while crossing a street on the University of Alabama in Huntsville campus. The *Huntsville Business Journal* reported that the collision caused permanent brain damage and was followed by an Alzheimer’s diagnosis. Li did not return to work afterwards. Her husband, who witnessed the collision, suffered a heart attack at the scene and died the following year.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

Men then described an extended period that is crucial to reconstructing Li’s whereabouts. He said that he brought his mother home and personally cared for her for the next six years. His explanation was explicitly familial: she was his mother, she had enabled him to come to the United States and obtain an education, and he admired her. The interview also contains details characteristic of day-to-day caregiving rather than a second-hand reconstruction. Men recalled trying different kinds of music to comfort her and finding that Christian hymns appeared to reach her when other music did not.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

These details cannot independently establish every medical fact about Li’s condition, but they give the supposedly “missing” scientist a location within an ordinary domestic setting during the final years of her life. The chronology is also internally straightforward: the 2014 injury ended her working life; her husband died in 2015; Men cared for her for approximately six years; and Li died in July 2021. That sequence is consistent with the length of care Men reported.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

The public death record provides an important endpoint. Berryhill Funeral Home recorded Li as a Huntsville resident who died on 27 July 2021 and named “Dr. George Men” as her surviving son. It announced visitation and a funeral at Berryhill on 1 August, followed by burial at Faith Memorial Park.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.berryhillfh.com/guestbook/ning-li" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: berryhillfh.com">[Berryhill Funeral Home]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">berryhillfh.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Berryhill Funeral Home Ning Li Obituary</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Berryhill Funeral HomeNing Li Obituary July 27, 2021 - Berryhill Funeral Home &amp; CrematoryJuly 27, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 27, 2021</span></span></span> A separate Alabama-area obituary notice published on 29 July likewise reported that Ning Li of Madison, Alabama, had died on 27 July, named George Men as her son and repeated the Huntsville funeral arrangements.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://quadcitiesdaily.com/ning-li/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: quadcitiesdaily.com">[Quad Cities Daily]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">quadcitiesdaily.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Quad Cities Daily Ning Li – Quad Cities Daily</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Quad Cities Daily Ning Li – Quad Cities Daily</span></span></span>

## How six years of caregiving fill the missing chronology

The significance of Men’s account becomes clearer when it is compared with the older “disappearance” narrative. Li had indeed become extremely difficult for outsiders to follow. The *Huntsville Business Journal* found that her public publication trail largely stopped after the early 2000s, although she was still presenting related research at a 2003 gravitational-wave conference. In 2004, journalist Tim Ventura was already trying to “track down” Li; physicist Eugene [Podkletnov]({{ 'podkletnov/' | relative_url }}) reportedly told him that she was alive and working with the US Department of Defense but could not discuss her work.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

The gap was subsequently given a much more dramatic interpretation. In a 2008 interview quoted by the newspaper, physicist Jack Sarfatti claimed that Li had “disappeared” and was allegedly back in China. That statement helped turn an absence of accessible information into a physical-disappearance story. Men directly contradicted it in 2023, telling the newspaper that his mother had not gone back to China or defected.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

The caregiving chronology is especially useful because it identifies a later period in which the distinction between **publicly inaccessible** and **physically missing** becomes difficult to ignore. Whatever uncertainty surrounds Li’s activities between her last public scientific appearances and 2014, Men places her at UAH in 2014 when the accident occurred and then describes caring for her through the years immediately preceding her 2021 death. The obituary then independently places her death and funeral in Alabama.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

This produces three different propositions that should not be collapsed into one:

* **Li disappeared from open scientific publication.** There is substantial support for this in the sparse public record after the early 2000s. Her 2003 conference appearance is one of the last clearly documented public scientific activities identified in later reporting; a surviving conference schedule independently lists Ning Li presenting “Measurability of AC Gravity Fields” in May 2003.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>
* **Li became difficult for professional contacts and outsiders to reach.** Contemporary correspondence described by the *Huntsville Business Journal* supports that narrower sense of disappearance.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>
* **Li physically vanished and her whereabouts were unknown even to her family.** Men’s account strongly challenges this version. By 2014 at the latest, he describes a specific local event followed by years in which he was personally responsible for her care.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

That distinction is the main evidential value of the six-year caregiving period. A mystery about unpublished research can remain a mystery without requiring a missing person.

## Which parts of the timeline have outside support

Men is the primary source for the most detailed account of Li’s post-accident life, so his testimony should not be presented as though every element has been independently documented. The surviving public evidence supports some parts of the chronology much better than others.

The strongest independent support concerns **identity, place and death**. Berryhill Funeral Home identifies Ning Li as a Huntsville resident, gives her death date as 27 July 2021 and identifies George Men as her surviving son.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.berryhillfh.com/guestbook/ning-li" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: berryhillfh.com">[Berryhill Funeral Home]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">berryhillfh.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Berryhill Funeral Home Ning Li Obituary</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Berryhill Funeral HomeNing Li Obituary July 27, 2021 - Berryhill Funeral Home &amp; CrematoryJuly 27, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 27, 2021</span></span></span> The *Quad Cities Daily* independently reproduced the death notice two days after her death, describing her as being from Madison, Alabama, and again identifying George Men as her son.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://quadcitiesdaily.com/ning-li/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: quadcitiesdaily.com">[Quad Cities Daily]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">quadcitiesdaily.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Quad Cities Daily Ning Li – Quad Cities Daily</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Quad Cities Daily Ning Li – Quad Cities Daily</span></span></span> These records are difficult to reconcile with the older claim that Li had simply returned to China and remained there.

There is also documentary context showing that Li had not vanished immediately after receiving Pentagon funding. Her company’s public history records AC Gravity LLC as an Alabama-registered foreign limited-liability company associated with a Huntsville address and Ning Li as its registered agent.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://al.ltddir.com/companies/ac-gravity-llc/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: al.ltddir.com">[Alabama Company Directory]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">al.ltddir.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Alabama Company Directory AC Gravity, LLC &#124; Alabama Company Directory</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">abama Company DirectoryAC Gravity, LLC &#124; Alabama Company DirectoryFebruary 28, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 28, 2026</span></span></span> More importantly for the chronology, an independently available programme for the May 2003 Gravitational-Wave Conference lists Dr Ning Li presenting paper HFGW-03-106, “Measurability of AC Gravity Fields”.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.integrityresearchinstitute.org/Gravity-Meeting-Schedule.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: integrityresearchinstitute.org">[Integrity Research Institute]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">integrityresearchinstitute.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on integrityresearchinstitute.org.</span></span></span> Those records reinforce the point that her withdrawal from conventional publication was gradual rather than evidence of an abrupt physical disappearance.

The **2014 accident and six-year caregiving period**, however, currently rest principally on Men’s testimony as reported by the *Huntsville Business Journal*. The newspaper says Li was struck by a vehicle on the UAH campus, suffered permanent brain damage, received an Alzheimer’s diagnosis, stopped working and was subsequently cared for at home by her son.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span> Later online accounts commonly repeat those details, but repetition downstream from the same 2023 interview is not independent corroboration. No publicly accessible police report, hospital record or contemporaneous UAH statement surfaced in the material reviewed here that independently establishes the collision and its medical consequences.

That limitation is important, but it does not put every part of Men’s account on equal footing. His relationship to Li is independently documented; Li’s continued association with Huntsville is documented at her death; her death date and local funeral arrangements are documented; and his description of approximately six years of caregiving fits the interval between the reported 2014 accident and her death in July 2021.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.berryhillfh.com/guestbook/ning-li" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: berryhillfh.com">[berryhillfh.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">berryhillfh.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Berryhill Funeral Home Ning Li Obituary</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Berryhill Funeral HomeNing Li Obituary July 27, 2021 - Berryhill Funeral Home &amp; CrematoryJuly 27, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 27, 2021</span></span></span> The evidence is therefore partly corroborated rather than wholly independent.

## What the caregiving account resolves — and what it does not

Men’s chronology resolves the most literal version of the Ning Li disappearance story much more effectively than it resolves the secrecy surrounding her scientific work. The distinction is essential in assessing allegations involving UFO, advanced-propulsion or [antigravity]({{ 'antigravity/' | relative_url }}) researchers. A person can disappear from journals, conferences and colleagues’ address books without disappearing from ordinary life.

For Li, the public scientific gap was real enough to invite speculation. Her AC Gravity research had received US defence funding, her public output subsequently dwindled, and outsiders struggled to contact her. The 2003 conference record confirms that her gravity-related work continued at least into that year.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.integrityresearchinstitute.org/Gravity-Meeting-Schedule.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: integrityresearchinstitute.org">[Integrity Research Institute]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">integrityresearchinstitute.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on integrityresearchinstitute.org.</span></span></span> The *Huntsville Business Journal* further reports Men’s claim that [classified work]({{ 'classified-work/' | relative_url }}) continued afterwards, although the details and results of that work remain outside the evidence supplied by the caregiving chronology.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

The [family account]({{ 'family-account/' | relative_url }}) therefore should not be stretched into proof about what Li was researching, what results she obtained, or why particular government records remain unavailable. Those are separate questions. Nor does evidence that she suffered an accident in 2014 explain every poorly documented year between her early-2000s public withdrawal and that accident.

What it does change is the evidential status of the word “missing”. By Men’s account, the researcher who had become mysterious to the internet was, during her final six years, a severely impaired mother being cared for by her son in Alabama. Her 2021 obituary then publicly connected that family to a named woman, a local death and local funeral arrangements.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

For disappearance narratives, that is a consequential correction. The unresolved story is no longer principally **where Ning Li went**. The defensible remaining question is narrower: **what happened in the poorly documented portion of her research career before the 2014 accident?** George Men’s caregiving chronology does not answer that question, but it removes the need to turn Li’s final years themselves into an unexplained disappearance.
