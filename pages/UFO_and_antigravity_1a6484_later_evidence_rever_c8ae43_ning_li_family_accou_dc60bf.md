## Introduction

For years, physicist Ning Li’s disappearance from public scientific life was treated in [UFO and antigravity]({{ 'ufo-and-antigravity/' | relative_url }}) circles as a literal disappearance. The most important evidence against that story arrived surprisingly late. In July 2023, Huntsville journalist Noah Logan located Li’s son, Dr George Guangyu Men, through information that ultimately traced back to Li’s 2021 obituary. Men then supplied the first detailed public family chronology of the years that had previously been filled largely by speculation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

His account did not resolve what Li’s classified research achieved, nor independently prove every detail of her government work. It did, however, radically change the more basic question of what happened to Li herself. According to Men, she remained in the United States, continued working after disappearing from academic publication, rejected an approach to return to China, suffered a catastrophic road accident in 2014 and spent her final six years being cared for by her son before dying in Alabama in 2021.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

That distinction matters. The family testimony does not solve the mystery of Li’s research. It substantially dismantles the older mystery of the supposedly vanished scientist.

## How George Men was located in 2023

The route to Men began not with a classified document but with an obituary. Berryhill Funeral Home recorded that Ning Li died on 27 July 2021 and named her surviving son as Dr George Men, together with two grandchildren. It also scheduled a funeral and burial in the Huntsville area. A contemporary local death notice likewise identified George Men as her son. These mundane records were already difficult to reconcile with versions of the story in which Li had secretly defected to China or vanished without trace.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.berryhillfh.com/guestbook/ning-li" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: berryhillfh.com">[Berryhill Funeral Home]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">berryhillfh.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Berryhill Funeral Home Ning Li Obituary</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Berryhill Funeral HomeNing Li Obituary July 27, 2021 - Berryhill Funeral Home &amp; Crematory...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 27, 2021</span></span></span>

Logan, writing for the<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal investigation]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>, used the obituary’s identification of “George Guangyu Men” to search further. He reported finding a Huntsville business registered under Men’s name and an associated telephone number. Men answered the call and agreed to meet him.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

That meeting produced something the preceding internet discussion had conspicuously lacked: testimony from a close relative who said he had remained involved in Li’s life throughout the supposedly mysterious period. Men told Logan that enthusiasts had previously sent him letters from places as distant as New Zealand seeking information about his mother’s work. Yet he had only a vague idea of how large the online disappearance mythology had become. Logan showed him a popular video presenting Li as a scientist who had discovered [antigravity]({{ 'antigravity/' | relative_url }}) and then disappeared; Men’s reaction became the starting point for correcting its chronology.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

The significance of the interview therefore lies less in Men being an independent scientific authority than in his position as a direct witness to Li’s personal life. Internet investigators had been attempting to infer her whereabouts from gaps in publications, unanswered emails and rumours among physicists. Men could instead describe where his mother was, what happened within the family and how her working life ended.

## The chronology Men gave for Li’s later life

Men’s account divides the supposed “missing years” into two very different periods: a working period in which Li had become publicly silent, followed after 2014 by years of severe disability.

The first period directly contradicts the most influential defection story. In a 2008 interview, physicist Jack Sarfatti had claimed that Li had disappeared, returned to China and could no longer be reached by people at the Pentagon. Logan’s article reproduced that allegation and then put the underlying issue to Men. His answer was categorical: his mother had not gone back to China and had not defected. According to his account, she remained connected to US Department of Defense work.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

Men did, however, remember a China-related episode in 2008, which may help explain how a distorted version of events could have sounded plausible. He said visiting Chinese officials approached Li in the United States and wanted her to return to China. According to Men, she refused. He further recalled that when Li later wanted to travel to China following her mother’s death, the US government did not permit the trip. Men remembered his mother being deeply upset about it.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

The important evidential point is that Men’s version contains an element resembling the old rumour — Chinese interest in Li — while reversing its central conclusion. In his account, China tried to recruit her and she declined; she did not disappear into a Chinese programme.

Men also offered an explanation for the abrupt change in Li’s public behaviour. He remembered that while working at the University of Alabama in Huntsville she had enjoyed publishing her research. After moving into [classified work]({{ 'classified-work/' | relative_url }}) and obtaining what he described as a top-secret clearance, she could no longer discuss her findings. Men said she became noticeably quieter and sometimes returned home looking exhausted. When he once tried asking her about the work, her response, as he remembered it, was essentially that he knew nothing and should forget anything he thought he knew.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

That recollection fits one independently documented feature of the earlier chronology: Li really did move from academic research into defence-funded work. The award was due to finish in September 2002.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.zpenergy.com/modules.php?file=print&amp;name=News&amp;sid=838" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: zpenergy.com">[ZPEnergy]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">zpenergy.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Gravity-Waves &amp; Missing Scientists at American Antigravity.ComJuly 19, 2004...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 19, 2004</span></span></span>

It does **not**, however, independently verify Men’s description of her subsequent security clearance or establish precisely what she worked on afterwards. Those parts of the chronology remain testimony rather than publicly documented programme history.

The second phase is much less mysterious. Men told Logan that in 2014 Li was struck by a vehicle while crossing a street on the University of Alabama in Huntsville campus. He said the collision caused permanent brain damage and was followed by an Alzheimer’s diagnosis. She did not return to work.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

The accident also devastated the family. According to Men’s account, Li’s husband of 46 years witnessed the collision and suffered a heart attack at the time; he died the following year. Men then brought his mother home and personally cared for her for the next six years. He explained his decision in family rather than scientific terms: she was his mother, had enabled him to build a life and education in the United States, and was someone he deeply admired.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

That final date is not dependent on Men’s testimony: Berryhill Funeral Home’s obituary independently records her death, identifies George Men as her son and gives funeral arrangements for 1 August, followed by burial at Faith Memorial Park.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.berryhillfh.com/guestbook/ning-li" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: berryhillfh.com">[Berryhill Funeral Home]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">berryhillfh.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Berryhill Funeral Home Ning Li Obituary</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Berryhill Funeral HomeNing Li Obituary July 27, 2021 - Berryhill Funeral Home &amp; Crematory...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 27, 2021</span></span></span>

## What Men’s testimony establishes — and what it does not

The strongest conclusion is narrower than some later retellings suggest. George Men’s testimony provides powerful evidence that Ning Li was not a conventional missing person and did not secretly relocate to China as the old story alleged. His account is especially persuasive on her personal whereabouts because he describes remaining involved in her life, caring for her for six years and arranging her final years in Alabama. Her 2021 obituary independently confirms her death there and his relationship to her.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

The chronology also gives a plausible explanation for why outsiders could mistake professional silence for physical disappearance. Li’s public scientific trail became exceptionally thin after defence funding entered the story. By 2004, an alternative-energy site was already describing her as a “[missing scientist]({{ 'missing-scientist/' | relative_url }})”, even while reproducing details of the genuine Army award to AC Gravity.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.zpenergy.com/modules.php?file=print&amp;name=News&amp;sid=838" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: zpenergy.com">[ZPEnergy]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">zpenergy.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Gravity-Waves &amp; Missing Scientists at American Antigravity.ComJuly 19, 2004...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 19, 2004</span></span></span> Later rumours transformed the absence of publications and contact information into assertions that she had returned to China. Men’s account separates those two propositions: she had disappeared **from public science**, he says, but had not disappeared **as a person**.

There are nevertheless important limits to what the 2023 interview proves. Men was reporting family memories years after the events. The public evidence located for this account does not include Li’s security-clearance documentation, employment records covering every year after 2003, the underlying classified project files, records documenting the alleged travel refusal, or primary records of the 2014 collision. The Huntsville Business Journal also reported that Freedom of Information Act requests concerning the results of the earlier Department of Defense grant had been denied.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

Consequently, claims such as “the government classified Li’s successful antigravity technology” go considerably beyond the available evidence. The documented Army award shows that the government funded an experiment intended to test Li’s ideas; it does not show that those ideas succeeded.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.zpenergy.com/modules.php?file=print&amp;name=News&amp;sid=838" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: zpenergy.com">[ZPEnergy]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">zpenergy.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Gravity-Waves &amp; Missing Scientists at American Antigravity.ComJuly 19, 2004...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 19, 2004</span></span></span> Men’s testimony, meanwhile, supports the proposition that his mother performed classified defence work and was restricted in what she could discuss, but it cannot by itself reveal the results of that work.

The same caution applies in the opposite direction. The ordinary explanation for Li’s personal disappearance from view does not demonstrate that every unanswered question about her research has been resolved. Her unpublished work remains precisely that: unpublished. The correct evidential update is therefore not that there was “nothing secret” about Ning Li, but that secrecy surrounding her professional activities was wrongly expanded into a much larger story about her physical whereabouts.

## The family account changed the mystery

George Men’s account is a particularly clear example of how entrenched UFO and antigravity lore can survive because different kinds of unknowns become fused together. Before 2023, there really were unanswered questions about Ning Li: why had she stopped publishing, what became of the defence-funded experiments, and why was she so difficult for former associates to contact? Those gaps were real. The leap from those gaps to “Li vanished” or “Li defected to China” was not comparably well established.

The 2023 interview broke that chain by supplying information from inside the family. It replaced a single dramatic blank — *scientist disappears after secret antigravity research* — with a sequence of ordinary human events: classified work, continued residence in the United States, an approach from Chinese officials that Men says Li rejected, a devastating accident, disability, family care and death in Alabama.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

That does not make Men’s testimony infallible, and the parts concerning classified government activity remain difficult to verify independently. But for the narrower question of Ning Li’s supposedly “missing years”, it shifts the burden of evidence decisively. Any claim that she physically disappeared, defected to China or met an unexplained fate now has to account for a named close relative giving a detailed contradictory chronology, together with contemporary death and funeral records placing the end of her life in the Huntsville area.

The remaining mystery is therefore substantially smaller than the legend. What became of Ning Li’s unpublished defence research is still uncertain. What happened to Ning Li herself is no longer comparably obscure.
