## Introduction

Chronology is one of the strongest tests available for claims that UFO or [antigravity]({{ 'antigravity/' | relative_url }}) researchers were deliberately silenced. A suppression theory is not strengthened merely because a scientist worked on an unusual subject and later died, disappeared from public view or stopped publishing. The proposed cause has to fit the order of events. Evidence of threats, surveillance, coercion or intervention should appear before, or at least plausibly during, the event it is supposed to explain. Facts that pre-date the alleged suppression mechanism can sharply weaken a later causal story.

Two cases show why this matters. James E. McDonald was genuinely prominent in UFO research, but the documented sequence includes an earlier suicide attempt before his death in June 1971. Ning Li really did vanish from public scientific discussion after classified defence work, yet later family reporting established that she remained in the United States, suffered a catastrophic road accident in 2014 and died in 2021. In both cases, putting the dates back into order changes what the evidence can reasonably support.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://files.ncas.org/mccarthy/obit.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: files.ncas.org">[ncas.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">files.ncas.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Files Mc Carthy Dissertation: James E. ## Why sequence matters in causal claims

A chronology test asks a simple question: **does the alleged cause occur at the right time to explain the alleged effect?** In this subject, that means distinguishing several different events which are often compressed into a single dramatic narrative: controversial research, professional criticism, government interest, loss of publication, personal crisis, accident, disappearance from public view, death and finally the later emergence of a conspiracy interpretation.

That distinction matters because those events are not interchangeable. Government interest in a scientist's work is evidence of government interest. A security clearance may explain why publications stop. Professional [hostility]({{ 'hostility/' | relative_url }}) may explain career difficulties. None of those facts, by themselves, establishes homicide. Likewise, a mysterious gap in the public record is not automatically evidence that the person was physically missing.

A useful chronology therefore separates at least four questions:

## McDonald’s earlier suicide attempt changes the evidential picture

James E. McDonald is an important test case because the basic ingredients of a suppression narrative are genuinely present. He was an established atmospheric scientist at the University of Arizona, investigated UFO reports intensively during the 1960s and publicly criticised both governmental and scientific handling of the subject. The University of Arizona preserves an extensive McDonald collection containing correspondence, UFO case files, [Project Blue Book]({{ 'blue-book/' | relative_url }}) material, interviews and records of his disputes over official investigations. His 1971 American Meteorological Society obituary likewise noted that his UFO work brought him into conflict with other scientists and engineers.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://lib.arizona.edu/special-collections/collections/james-e-mcdonald-papers" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lib.arizona.edu">[University of Arizona Libraries]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lib.arizona.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on arizona.edu.</span></span></span>

If the chronology stopped there — prominent scientist, outspoken critic, conflict with institutions, death at 51 — the outline could appear highly suggestive. It does not stop there.

McDonald died on 13 June 1971. Contemporary biographical sources identify the death as suicide, and later accounts describe a revolver and an apparent suicide note being found with him. Crucially, accounts of his final months also record an unsuccessful suicide attempt earlier in 1971, before the fatal event.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://files.ncas.org/mccarthy/obit.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: files.ncas.org">[ncas.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">files.ncas.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Files Mc Carthy Dissertation: James E. Mc Donald Obituary</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">NCAS FilesMcCarthy Dissertation: James E. McDonald Obituary...</span></span></span>

That earlier attempt is not a peripheral detail. It changes the causal comparison. A murder theory has to account not merely for a suspicious-looking death but for a documented pattern of self-harm already under way before the final death. The chronology therefore supplies an alternative causal pathway that exists independently of any claimed assassination scenario.

The strongest version of a suppression claim would need positive evidence that the earlier attempt was itself staged, misreported or connected to coercion. Merely pointing to McDonald's disputes with official bodies does not do that. Institutional resistance and personal crisis can coexist, and the existence of the former does not erase evidence for the latter.

This is exactly where chronology prevents retrospective compression. Later summaries sometimes jump directly from McDonald's UFO advocacy to his death, creating an implied sequence of **research → official hostility → elimination**. The fuller sequence is closer to **research and public controversy → severe personal crisis and an earlier suicide attempt → subsequent death by suicide**. That does not settle every historical question about the pressures he faced, but it makes the specific claim of homicide substantially harder to sustain without additional evidence.

## Ning Li’s “disappearance” looks different when dated properly

Ning Li illustrates a different chronological problem: the transformation of **loss of public visibility** into an apparent physical disappearance.

Li conducted highly unconventional work on claimed gravitational effects associated with superconductors at the University of Alabama in Huntsville. She left the university environment, established AC Gravity and obtained US Department of Defense funding. According to later reporting, the company received a $448,970 award in 2001. Her public publication trail then largely stopped, while evidence indicates that she continued work connected to the defence sector.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

That gap is real and is one reason the case became so attractive to suppression theories. But the dates show that “she stopped publishing”, “she was working under secrecy restrictions” and “she physically disappeared” are three different propositions.

The Huntsville Business Journal reconstructed the sequence in 2023 through an interview with Li's son, George Guangyu Men. Its account states that Li was still alive and working with the Department of Defense after her academic publication record had gone quiet. It cites a 2003 technical presentation and a 2004 exchange in which physicist Eugene [Podkletnov]({{ 'podkletnov/' | relative_url }}) reportedly said that Li remained alive and engaged in defence work but could not discuss it.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

The mythology intensified later. The same reporting identifies a 2008 interview in which physicist Jack Sarfatti claimed that Li had disappeared and possibly returned to China. That assertion proved especially influential because it supplied a dramatic explanation for an otherwise mundane evidential gap: people could no longer reach a scientist whose publications had ceased.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

Her son's account, however, directly contradicted the physical-disappearance story. He said she had not defected to China and had remained in the United States. He also described her work as classified after she obtained a high-level security clearance, offering a straightforward explanation for at least part of the public silence: she was no longer operating under normal academic publication conditions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

The next crucial date is **2014**. According to the family account published by the Huntsville Business Journal, Li was struck by a vehicle while crossing a street on the University of Alabama in Huntsville campus. The collision caused permanent brain damage, she was subsequently diagnosed with Alzheimer's disease, and she never returned to work. Her husband witnessed the accident, suffered a heart attack at the time and died the following year. Li then lived under her son's care for several years.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

Li died on **27 July 2021**. Her funeral-home obituary confirms that date and identifies her as a scientist associated with superconductivity and antigravity research.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.berryhillfh.com/guestbook/ning-li" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: berryhillfh.com">[Berryhill Funeral Home]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">berryhillfh.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Berryhill Funeral Home Ning Li Obituary</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Berryhill Funeral HomeNing Li Obituary July 27, 2021 - Berryhill Funeral Home &amp; CrematoryJuly 27, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 27, 2021</span></span></span>

That sequence substantially alters the disappearance narrative:

**1999–2001:** Li leaves academia, creates AC Gravity and receives defence funding.

**2003–2004:** surviving evidence places her alive and still associated with defence work.

**2008:** a public claim circulates that she has disappeared or returned to China.

**2014:** she suffers a severe road accident in Huntsville.

**2014–2021:** she lives with profound cognitive impairment under family care.

**27 July 2021:** she dies.

**2023:** reporting based on her son reconstructs the missing years and publicly corrects the disappearance story.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

The crucial point is that the **disappearance narrative arose before the public had an accurate account of her private life**. A gap in public information was converted into a claim about what had happened to her. Once later evidence supplied those missing dates, the theory had to change.

## What Li’s chronology does — and does not — resolve

The corrected timeline does not eliminate every legitimate question about Li's work. There remains an important distinction between the fate of the scientist and the fate of her research.

Her public scientific output did decline sharply after she entered defence-associated work. The Huntsville Business Journal reported that requests for records from the 2001 defence grant had been denied and quoted her son as saying that after receiving a security clearance she could no longer discuss her work as she had in academia. Those facts support the much narrower proposition that some of Li's later research was not publicly accessible.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

They do **not**, without further evidence, establish that she was abducted, killed, forced to defect or deliberately removed from public life. Those are different claims requiring different evidence.

The 2014 collision deserves the same discipline. Because it ended her working life, it can look sinister when inserted into a suppression narrative. But chronological proximity alone is not evidence of intent. The reported facts establish an accident with devastating consequences. To reclassify it as a deliberate attack would require independent evidence about the driver, circumstances, planning, threats or other indicators of intent. The available family reporting does not provide that evidence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

Chronology therefore narrows the unresolved question. Instead of “Why did Ning Li disappear?”, the more defensible question is “What precisely became of the results of her later classified or defence-funded research?” The first premise has largely been corrected by subsequent evidence; the second remains partly opaque.

## Later stories can reverse cause and effect

Researcher-suppression narratives are particularly vulnerable to what might be called **retrospective causal stitching**: separate events are linked after the fact because their combination makes a coherent story.

The pattern is easy to see. A scientist works on an unusual subject. Publications stop. Years later, someone describes the scientist as missing. Still later, an accident or death becomes publicly known. The final narrative may then be told as though observers at the time saw a continuous sequence of threats, disappearance and death.

But the historical record may show something different: the supposed “disappearance” was originally an inference from silence; the accident occurred years later; and the explanatory family evidence emerged later still.

Ning Li's case demonstrates this unusually clearly. The 2008 claim that she had disappeared and returned to China preceded the 2014 accident by about six years and was later contradicted by her son. Therefore the accident cannot explain why the 2008 disappearance story arose. The story had already been created out of an information gap.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

McDonald presents the reverse problem. His institutional conflicts came before his death and are therefore chronologically eligible as background pressures. But so did his earlier suicide attempt. Any theory that highlights the first chronology while omitting the second is not testing causation consistently.

These examples show why exact ordering is more informative than a collection of suggestive coincidences. A credible hypothesis should survive the addition of inconvenient dates.

## A practical timeline test for suppression claims

For alleged suspicious deaths or disappearances in UFO and unconventional-propulsion research, the most useful test is to build the sequence before assigning motive.

Start with the earliest independently documented point at which the researcher was alive, active and publicly traceable. Add dated publications, employment changes, grants, security restrictions, known threats, medical events, accidents, missing-person reports, police findings, death records and later witness accounts. Keep contemporary documents separate from recollections recorded years afterwards.

Then ask three questions.

**First, was there actually an unexplained event at the time?** Li's case shows why this matters. She became difficult for outsiders to trace, but the later family account says she was never officially a missing person. Public silence and physical disappearance should not be merged.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

**Second, does the proposed suppression mechanism pre-date the outcome?** Evidence discovered only later can still illuminate earlier events, but a causal theory should identify when the alleged coercion began and how it operated. Vague claims that “the government was interested” are too broad to explain a particular death.

**Third, does the theory explain competing evidence better than ordinary alternatives do?** McDonald's earlier suicide attempt is exactly this kind of competing evidence. Li's classified employment and later catastrophic injury provide [competing explanations]({{ 'competing-explanations/' | relative_url }}) for her publication silence and withdrawal from professional life. A suppression theory must account for those facts rather than treating them as distractions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_E._McDonald" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">James E</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">McDonald - WikipediaJames E. McDonald - Wikipedia James E. McDonald James Edward McDonald Born (1920-05-07) May 7, 1920 Duluth, Minnesota...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 7, 1920</span></span></span>

The result may still be uncertainty. That is not a failure of the method. It is often the most accurate outcome when records are incomplete.



<figure class="article-media-figure article-media-figure-illustration"><img src="{{ "/assets/images/UFO_and_antigravity_1a6484_chronology_test_4f29a5-Illustration-3-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Timeline Test illustration 3" data-theme-src-dark="{{ "/assets/images/UFO_and_antigravity_1a6484_chronology_test_4f29a5-Illustration-3-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{ "/assets/images/UFO_and_antigravity_1a6484_chronology_test_4f29a5-Illustration-3-light.svg" | relative_url }}" width="1920" height="1080" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low"><figcaption class="article-media-caption"><span class="article-media-caption-label">Explanatory illustration 3</span></figcaption></figure>

## Can the timeline survive the suppression theory?

For a researcher-suppression theory to become more than an inference from coincidence, its chronology should reveal a coherent chain: identifiable motive, documented pressure or intervention, a plausible opportunity window and an outcome that is not already better explained by established prior events.

On the two clearest chronology tests here, the sweeping version of the theory performs poorly.

With James E. McDonald, the record confirms both serious institutional conflict over UFO research and an earlier suicide attempt before his death. The first fact makes his professional experience relevant to the history of stigma around UFO research; the second substantially weakens attempts to portray his death as unexplained elimination.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://files.ncas.org/mccarthy/obit.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: files.ncas.org">[ncas.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">files.ncas.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Files Mc Carthy Dissertation: James E. Mc Donald Obituary</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">NCAS FilesMcCarthy Dissertation: James E. McDonald Obituary...</span></span></span>

With Ning Li, the central mystery was largely created by missing public information. Defence-related secrecy appears to have limited what she could publish or discuss, but later evidence places her alive in the United States, followed by a documented 2014 accident, years of severe impairment and death in 2021. The chronology therefore supports secrecy around aspects of her research far more readily than it supports a claim that she herself was secretly disappeared.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

That distinction is the main value of the timeline test. It does not require assuming that institutions are always transparent or that every unusual death is fully understood. It simply requires that a causal claim respect the order in which events actually occurred. In this subject, adding the missing dates often makes the story less dramatic — but considerably more informative.
