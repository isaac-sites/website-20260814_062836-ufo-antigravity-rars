## Introduction

Ning Li’s obituary did not solve every question surrounding the physicist’s abrupt retreat from public scientific life, but it changed the nature of the mystery. For years, Li — known for controversial research into possible gravitational effects involving superconductors — was routinely described online as a scientist who had “disappeared” after receiving US defence funding. The Berryhill Funeral Home obituary established a firm endpoint: Li remained associated with the Huntsville, Alabama, area and died there on 27 July 2021. It named her surviving son, George Men, and announced a local funeral and burial.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.berryhillfh.com/obituary/ning-li?lud=4CF765EE88E7526FCBA619C30101F7E6" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: berryhillfh.com">[Berryhill Funeral Home]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">berryhillfh.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Berryhill Funeral Home Ning Li Obituary</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Berryhill Funeral HomeNing Li Obituary July 27, 2021 - Berryhill Funeral Home &amp; CrematoryJuly 27, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 27, 2021</span></span></span>



<figure class="article-media-figure article-media-figure-illustration"><img src="{{ "/assets/images/UFO_and_antigravity_1a6484_obituary_evidence_4a5b65_ning_li_disappearanc_1d6afe-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Ning Li illustration 1" data-theme-src-dark="{{ "/assets/images/UFO_and_antigravity_1a6484_obituary_evidence_4a5b65_ning_li_disappearanc_1d6afe-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{ "/assets/images/UFO_and_antigravity_1a6484_obituary_evidence_4a5b65_ning_li_disappearanc_1d6afe-Illustration-1-light.svg" | relative_url }}" width="1920" height="1080" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low"><figcaption class="article-media-caption"><span class="article-media-caption-label">Explanatory illustration 1</span></figcaption></figure>

That information mattered because it created a bridge between an internet mystery and Li’s family. In 2023, the *Huntsville Business Journal* interviewed Men and produced a much fuller chronology. His account was that Li had not defected to China or vanished into unknown custody. She continued defence-related work, ceased publishing after receiving a security clearance, suffered devastating injuries in a 2014 traffic accident and spent her remaining years being cared for by her son.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

The result is a useful case study in what an obituary can — and cannot — establish when a researcher’s death or disappearance has become entangled with UFO, [antigravity]({{ 'antigravity/' | relative_url }}) and government-secrecy narratives.

## Why Ning Li was described as having disappeared

The disappearance story grew from a genuine break in Li’s public scientific record. During the 1990s she was a visible researcher at the University of Alabama in Huntsville (UAH), publishing with physicist Douglas Torr on proposed links between superconductivity and gravitomagnetic effects. Her work attracted enough attention to appear in mainstream science and technology coverage. A 1998 *Wired* investigation, for example, discussed Li and Torr's theories while also presenting the substantial scientific objections to gravity-shielding claims.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.wired.com/1998/03/antigravity/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: wired.com">[WIRED]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">wired.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Breaking the Law of Gravity &#124; WIREDMarch 1, 1998...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 1, 1998</span></span></span>

Li then moved away from normal academic visibility. She left UAH and established AC Gravity LLC in 1999. In 2001 the company received a Department of Defense award reported as $448,970 for further work. Afterwards, the expected public trail of papers and experimental results largely stopped. The *Huntsville Business Journal* found that Li published nothing after 2002, although AC Gravity remained registered for years.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

Her public record did not end instantaneously. A programme for a May 2003 gravitational-wave conference lists Li presenting a paper entitled “Measurability of AC Gravity Fields”.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.integrityresearchinstitute.org/Gravity-Meeting-Schedule.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: integrityresearchinstitute.org">[Integrity Research Institute]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">integrityresearchinstitute.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">March 26, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 26, 2026</span></span></span> The *Huntsville Business Journal* also reported a private May 2003 email in which she discussed an experimental “output effect”. But after this period, people interested in her research had increasing difficulty contacting her.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

There was therefore a real informational gap, but “disappeared” gradually acquired a stronger meaning than the evidence warranted. An email exchange attributed to physicist Eugene [Podkletnov]({{ 'podkletnov/' | relative_url }}) and journalist Tim Ventura is particularly important because it shows that uncertainty about Li's accessibility existed as early as 2004. Podkletnov reportedly told Ventura that Li was well and working for the Department of Defense but was not permitted to discuss her work openly. He said she had telephoned him that year, although he did not have her current work email address.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://theethicsofmadness.blogspot.com/2013/08/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theethicsofmadness.blogspot.com">[The Ethics of Madness]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theethicsofmadness.blogspot.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Ethics of MadnessThe Ethics of Madness: August 2013August 25, 2013...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 25, 2013</span></span></span>

If authentic and accurately preserved, that exchange cuts against the idea that Li had literally vanished immediately after her defence grant. It instead suggests something more specific: she had become difficult for former professional contacts to reach while remaining alive and engaged in defence work.

The story took a more dramatic turn in 2008, when physicist Jack Sarfatti publicly claimed that Li had disappeared, returned to China and could no longer be reached by people at the Pentagon. The allegation subsequently circulated through [UFO and antigravity]({{ 'ufo-and-antigravity/' | relative_url }}) discussions and became a central component of the Li mystery.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

There was an obvious narrative appeal. A scientist publicly proposes an extraordinary propulsion-related technology, obtains military funding, stops publishing and then becomes inaccessible. But those facts did not demonstrate abduction, assassination, defection or suppression. What the surviving record demonstrated was a loss of **public visibility**. Over time, that narrower fact was frequently transformed into a claim of physical disappearance.

## What the obituary established about her final years

The Berryhill Funeral Home notice supplied the first simple, independently accessible fact capable of changing that narrative: Ning Li had died in Alabama.

The notice identifies her as Dr Ning Li of Huntsville and gives her dates as 14 January 1943 to 27 July 2021. It specifically connects her identity to superconductivity and antigravity research and says that she had constructed a 12-inch high-temperature superconducting disc in the late 1990s. Most importantly for the disappearance question, it names her son, Dr George Men, and grandchildren. It schedules visitation and a funeral at Berryhill Funeral Home on 1 August 2021, followed by burial at Faith Memorial Park.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.berryhillfh.com/guestbook/ning-li" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: berryhillfh.com">[Berryhill Funeral Home]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">berryhillfh.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Berryhill Funeral Home Ning Li Obituary</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Berryhill Funeral HomeNing Li Obituary July 27, 2021 - Berryhill Funeral Home &amp; CrematoryJuly 27, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 27, 2021</span></span></span>

A separate Alabama death notice published on 29 July similarly reported that Ning Li had died on 27 July and named George Men and her grandchildren, while giving the same funeral and burial arrangements. Interestingly, it gives her age as 78, whereas Berryhill's prose says 79; the birth and death dates printed by Berryhill would make her 78. That minor inconsistency is a reminder that [obituaries]({{ 'obituaries/' | relative_url }}) should not automatically be treated as error-free official records.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.berryhillfh.com/obituary/ning-li?lud=4CF765EE88E7526FCBA619C30101F7E6" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: berryhillfh.com">[Berryhill Funeral Home]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">berryhillfh.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Berryhill Funeral Home Ning Li Obituary</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Berryhill Funeral HomeNing Li Obituary July 27, 2021 - Berryhill Funeral Home &amp; CrematoryJuly 27, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 27, 2021</span></span></span>

For the disappearance question, however, the important details are mutually consistent. Li had family in Alabama, died there, and received an ordinary local funeral. Nothing in the notice supports the longstanding allegation that she had secretly relocated to China.

The obituary nevertheless had significant limitations. It did not explain what Li had been doing during the long interval after her last public scientific appearances. It did not describe the circumstances that ended her career. It did not address the defence work, security classification or rumours surrounding her whereabouts. And although it says she “passed peacefully away”, that phrase is not a medical finding or a cause-of-death determination.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.berryhillfh.com/obituary/ning-li?lud=4CF765EE88E7526FCBA619C30101F7E6" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: berryhillfh.com">[Berryhill Funeral Home]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">berryhillfh.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Berryhill Funeral Home Ning Li Obituary</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Berryhill Funeral HomeNing Li Obituary July 27, 2021 - Berryhill Funeral Home &amp; CrematoryJuly 27, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 27, 2021</span></span></span>

In other words, the obituary closed one question while leaving another open. **Where did the supposedly missing Ning Li ultimately turn up?** Huntsville. **Why had almost nobody in the scientific community heard from her for so long?** The obituary itself could not answer that.

Its unexpected importance was that it supplied the name of somebody who could.

## How family reporting filled the missing chronology

In July 2023, journalist Noah Logan published an investigation in the *Huntsville Business Journal* after locating and interviewing Li’s son, George Guangyu Men. Logan explicitly distinguished between Li’s internet “disappearance” and an actual missing-person case: he reported that she had never been legally or officially missing.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

Men's account provided a chronology that substantially changed the interpretation of the surviving evidence.

According to Men, his mother did **not** leave the United States for China. He said she continued working with the Department of Defense and that obtaining a top-secret security clearance prevented her from continuing the open scientific publishing and conference activity she had previously enjoyed. Men recalled that she became markedly quieter about her work after moving from university research into the classified environment.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

That account fits an important piece of evidence predating the disappearance mythology: Podkletnov's reported 2004 statement that Li was alive, working for the Department of Defense and unable to discuss the work openly. The two accounts are separated by nearly two decades and are not equivalent in evidential quality, but they point in the same direction.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://theethicsofmadness.blogspot.com/2013/08/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theethicsofmadness.blogspot.com">[The Ethics of Madness]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theethicsofmadness.blogspot.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Ethics of MadnessThe Ethics of Madness: August 2013August 25, 2013...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 25, 2013</span></span></span>

Men also supplied an explanation for the China story. He told the *Huntsville Business Journal* that Chinese officials approached Li in 2008 and wanted her to return, but that she rejected the proposal. This directly contradicts the contemporary allegation that she had already defected or returned to China. Men further recalled that when Li later wanted to travel to China for her mother's funeral, she was denied permission to make the trip.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

## What remains mysterious — and what does not

The strongest available evidence substantially weakens the literal “[missing scientist]({{ 'missing-scientist/' | relative_url }})” interpretation of Ning Li's story. Her lack of publications and public appearances was real. Difficulty reaching her was real. Her connection to US defence-funded research was real. But those facts are not evidence that she physically disappeared.

Indeed, one piece of evidence from the period when she was supposedly missing said precisely the opposite: Podkletnov's reported 2004 message described her as alive and working for the Department of Defense.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://theethicsofmadness.blogspot.com/2013/08/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theethicsofmadness.blogspot.com">[The Ethics of Madness]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theethicsofmadness.blogspot.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Ethics of MadnessThe Ethics of Madness: August 2013August 25, 2013...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 25, 2013</span></span></span> Later, the funeral-home obituary placed the end of her life firmly back in the Huntsville community.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.berryhillfh.com/obituary/ning-li?lud=4CF765EE88E7526FCBA619C30101F7E6" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: berryhillfh.com">[Berryhill Funeral Home]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">berryhillfh.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Berryhill Funeral Home Ning Li Obituary</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Berryhill Funeral HomeNing Li Obituary July 27, 2021 - Berryhill Funeral Home &amp; CrematoryJuly 27, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 27, 2021</span></span></span> Finally, her son's 2023 account supplied a coherent explanation for both phases of her silence: [classified work]({{ 'classified-work/' | relative_url }}) followed by catastrophic injury and cognitive decline.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

What remains unresolved is primarily **the history of her research**, rather than her personal whereabouts. Publicly visible Freedom of Information Act activity confirms continuing attempts to obtain records concerning AC Gravity and its 2001 defence award. A Department of Defense Inspector General FOIA log, for example, records a July 2022 request for declassified records concerning AC Gravity and its grant.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://media.defense.gov/2025/Mar/20/2003673003/-1/-1/1/FOIA%20CASE%20LOG%20FISCAL%20YEAR%202022%20%28REDACTED%29.PDF" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: media.defense.gov">[U.S. Department of War]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">media.defense.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">FOIA CASE LOG FISCAL YEAR 2022 (REDACTED</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">U.S. Department of WarFreedom of Information Act Case Log Fiscal Year 2022 (Redacted)April 10, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 10, 2023</span></span></span> MuckRock also preserves requests seeking paperwork connected with the $448,970 award.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.muckrock.com/foi/united-states-of-america-10/department-of-defense-grant-for-ac-gravity-llc-2001-department-of-defense-defense-security-cooperation-agency-135084/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: muckrock.com">[MuckRock]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">muckrock.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Department of Defense Grant for AC Gravity LLC (2001) (Department of Defense, Defense Security Cooperation Agency) • MuckRockOcto...</span></span></span>

The *Huntsville Business Journal* journalist reported that his own requests for the grant results were denied and said this was consistent with earlier attempts.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span> That leaves legitimate historical questions about what work was performed, what results were produced, what became classified and why Li's later defence-related career left so little accessible documentation. A lack of publicly released records, however, does not by itself establish that Li achieved working antigravity technology or that authorities suppressed a successful breakthrough.

Nor does the evidence currently available turn Li's 2014 collision into a demonstrated suspicious attack. The crucial detailed account comes from her son and describes a vehicle collision followed by severe neurological consequences. The available obituary does not describe the accident at all, while the accessible reporting supplies no police finding, court record or other primary evidence demonstrating deliberate harm. Treating the collision as an assassination would therefore go substantially beyond the evidence reviewed here.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

That distinction is especially important when Li is placed in lists of allegedly dead or disappeared UFO and advanced-propulsion researchers. Her case contains an unusual combination of speculative physics, genuine defence funding, restricted information and years of public silence. Those ingredients help explain why the story acquired a conspiratorial interpretation. They do not establish that her disappearance or death was caused by her research.

## How the obituary changed the mystery

Ning Li's case demonstrates an unusually powerful but limited use of an obituary as historical evidence. The Berryhill notice did not reveal secret research, settle questions about AC Gravity or reconstruct Li's classified career. What it did was more basic and, for the disappearance claim, more consequential: it restored ordinary biographical facts to a story that had become detached from them.

Li had a documented death date. She had a son and grandchildren. Her funeral took place in Huntsville and her burial followed locally. The scientist whom internet accounts had repeatedly imagined as having defected to China or vanished into an unknown government programme had, at the end of her life, remained embedded in the Alabama community where her unusual scientific career had unfolded.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.berryhillfh.com/obituary/ning-li?lud=4CF765EE88E7526FCBA619C30101F7E6" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: berryhillfh.com">[Berryhill Funeral Home]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">berryhillfh.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Berryhill Funeral Home Ning Li Obituary</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Berryhill Funeral HomeNing Li Obituary July 27, 2021 - Berryhill Funeral Home &amp; CrematoryJuly 27, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 27, 2021</span></span></span>

The obituary then did something a death notice is not normally expected to do: by naming George Men, it gave a journalist the lead that eventually connected the public record with Li's private final years. The resulting 2023 interview transformed an apparent 18-year blank into a much more comprehensible sequence of classified work, family life, serious injury, long-term care and death.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://huntsvillebusinessjournal.com/news/2023/07/30/solving-the-mystery-of-huntsvilles-brilliant-scientist-disappearing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: huntsvillebusinessjournal.com">[Huntsville Business Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">huntsvillebusinessjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Huntsville Business JournalUncovering The Mystery Of Huntsville’s Brilliant Anti-gravity Scientist - Huntsville Business Journal...</span></span></span>

That does not make every part of Ning Li's story transparent. Her post-academic research remains poorly documented in the public domain, and claims concerning classified work necessarily have evidential limits while the underlying records remain unavailable. But the disappearance question is considerably less mysterious than it once appeared. The best-supported reconstruction is not that Ning Li vanished and was later found dead. It is that **her public scientific presence disappeared long before she did** — and an ordinary local obituary eventually supplied the clue that allowed the two things to be separated.
